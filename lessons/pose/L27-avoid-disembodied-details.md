# L27 — Avoid disembodied detail shots

## TL;DR

When a plate is designed as an "editorial detail" (e.g., hands on object, jewelry close-up, fabric detail), keep at least **partial body context** in the frame — a sliver of shoulder, partial face, an arm leading into the detail. **Pure isolated body parts read as stock photo and provoke "who/what is this?" reactions.**

## When this lesson applies

- Plates designed to highlight a specific object, accessory, or texture
- Tight detail compositions in multi-plate photobook sets
- Any frame where the model's identity should remain readable

## Mistake (canonical case)

**Jira Wanderlust Vol 06 J05-P5 v1** — designed as "editorial detail · hands gripping the vintage steering wheel · face softly out of focus or out of frame." Result: image showed two disembodied hands on a steering wheel with no clear connection to the model. User feedback: *"ภาพนี้ใครขับ ทำใหม่ดีกว่า รู้สึกแปลกๆ"* ("who's driving in this image? redo, feels weird").

Root cause: Without the model's body in frame, the viewer cannot tell:
- Whose hands these are
- Whether this is even part of the same set/story
- Why the image exists

The image read as a generic stock photo of "hands on steering wheel" rather than "Jira in the driver's seat about to drive."

## Fix (canonical case)

**J05-P5 v2** — redesigned as "Jira sitting in the driver's seat, body 3/4 toward camera, face fully visible, hand on wheel, hand on sunglasses." Same wheel + interior context, but now with body and face anchoring the composition. Trin approved.

## Fix protocol

For every detail-style plate, ensure the frame includes ONE of:

1. **Partial face visible** (chest-up close-up with detail object in foreground)
2. **Sliver of shoulder + neck + jaw** leading into the detail
3. **Arm clearly continuing into the body** (not just hand floating in space)
4. **Reflection/shadow of the body** in the detail (e.g., face reflected on glass)

Detail framing is fine; **isolated body parts are not**.

## Anti-patterns

- ❌ "Hands on wheel · face out of frame"
- ❌ "Fingers at necklace · cropped at chin"
- ❌ "Bare back close-up · head out of frame"
- ❌ Any framing that would work as a stock photo of the object alone

✅ "Chest-up close-up with hands lifting a glass to her lips, eyes meeting camera over the rim"
✅ "Detail of fingertips at her own collarbone, jaw and lower face partially visible"
