# L30 — "Soft Veil" (named style · invoke ON-DEMAND only)

## TL;DR

**Soft Veil** = a named ZenityX look: a tasteful, artistic-suggestive thin-fabric effect — the soft contour of the body reads *faintly* through a thin SINGLE-LAYER top in natural light. Sexy-as-art, **not** explicit. Achieved with ZERO explicit words → filter-safe.

> **Invoke ONLY when Trin says "Soft Veil" / "สไตล์ Soft Veil". NEVER auto-apply** — suggestive wardrobe is opt-in (don't pre-emptively safe-ify NOR pre-emptively sexy-ify; both are bias traps per VOCAB anti-pattern). Default level = "V2" (visible-but-soft, tasteful).

## The recipe — 2 keys (both filter-safe, NO explicit terms)

**Key 1 · Emergent suggestion** (let aesthetic + light do it, not words):
- Wardrobe: `soft WORN thin cotton tank/tee, low scoop neckline, full natural décolletage shown tastefully`
- Body: `full natural bust as a soft natural focal point — tasteful editorial, NOT crude`
- Skin: `real skin, soft pores, natural texture, gentle imperfections — NOT plastic-smooth, NOT filter-perfected, NOT airbrushed`
- Mood: `raw intimate Harley-Weir, tactile natural light, organic imperfection, 35mm film grain`
- Light: `soft natural light raking / pouring across her body and the thin fabric` (light across thin fabric is what reveals the contour)
- **NEVER** write `nipple / see-through / sheer / braless / wet` — Tier-1/explicit → Google filter block (per filter-craft-lv) AND unnecessary; the effect is emergent.

**Key 2 · Single-layer rule** (makes it look natural, not layered — Trin's insight):
- `worn as a SINGLE layer directly on skin · NO inner layer underneath · NO seams, edges, straps, or outline of any inner layer showing through the outer fabric · smooth uninterrupted single-layer drape over her natural form`
- **WHY:** if the model renders an inner garment, its seams press through the outer top → reads as two layers ("ไม่เนียน"). This phrasing fixes it AND avoids the Tier-1 words `bra` / `underwear` (which would trigger filter).

## Intensity dial (tune to the requested level)

| Axis | softer ← → stronger |
|---|---|
| **Light** | diffuse / soft frontal ← → **side / raking light** (the main driver) |
| **Fabric** | thicker tee / ribbed ← → thin worn tank |
| **Background** | busy / scene with atmosphere ← → plain / studio |

- **Default ("V2" sweet spot):** side light + thin tank **but in a scene with atmosphere** (NOT a plain studio) = "รางๆ artistic".
- Plain studio + hard side light + plain bg = **overshoots** (too defined) → avoid unless explicitly asked for stronger.

## Anti-patterns

- ❌ Inner-layer / bra seams visible through the outer fabric → use the single-layer rule (Key 2).
- ❌ Explicit words (`nipple/see-through/sheer/braless`) → filter block + crude. Never.
- ❌ Plain studio + hard side light → overshoots past "รางๆ".
- ❌ Auto-applying Soft Veil when not requested.
